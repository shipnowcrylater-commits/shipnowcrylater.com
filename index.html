Trust before Process

Why do so many teams run every ceremony in the agile handbook and still not trust each other? Ana and Egle talk to Marion, a UX-designer-turned-agile-coach and trained mediator, about the difference between performative process and the slower, harder work of actually building trust.

Ana Bernardo

Ana is a product and design leader who has spent over 10 years navigating complexity, building teams, and asking uncomfortable questions. She believes the hardest design challenges are rarely about the craft. They are about everything around it. That's why she started this podcast.

About the show

Design, leadership &
the stuff nobody tells you.

Ship Now, Cry Later is the podcast for designers and design leaders navigating the messy, exciting, sometimes chaotic reality of building great products. We talk about the hard calls, the unexpected growth moments, and the lessons that only come from doing the actual work. Roots before branches — always.
